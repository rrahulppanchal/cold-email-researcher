# Subject Line Playbook

Goal: make the right person feel "this is about me, I need to see this" - honestly. Relevance + curiosity, never deception.

## Rules recap
- 2-7 words, under ~40 characters
- Lowercase/sentence case, person-to-person feel
- Must name or point to THEM (business, town, product, competitor, review, page)
- Body must pay off the curiosity
- Banned: fake "Re:"/"Fwd:", ALL CAPS, "!!!", "FREE", "Guaranteed", "Act now", "100%", "$$$", "Limited time", "Urgent", clickbait with no payoff

## Formula families (give 5 options from different families)

### 1. Name + observation
- `{business}'s google menu`
- `{business} on mobile`
- `{product} pricing page`
- `the {business} booking button`

### 2. Curiosity / open loop
- `found this on {business}'s site`
- `noticed something about {business}`
- `1 thing on your homepage`
- `your 3rd google review`
- `about your contact page`

### 3. Competitor / local contrast
- `{competitor} vs {business}`
- `{business} vs {competitor} on google`
- `who shows up for "{service} {town}"`
- `{town} {service} search results`

### 4. Question (answerable, relevant)
- `who answers when you're on a job?`
- `still taking orders by DM?`
- `online quotes for {business}?`
- `ready for spring rush?`
- `{product} vs alternatives page?`

### 5. Lost money / missed opportunity
- `missed calls at {business}`
- `bookings after 6pm`
- `the zomato commission`
- `trial signups at {product}`

### 6. Gift / give-first
- `made a mockup for {business}`
- `{business} homepage idea`
- `3 fixes for {product}`
- `quick loom for {first_name}`

### 7. Trigger event
- `congrats on the new {location}`
- `{product} series A + your site`
- `saw the new menu`
- `re your post on {topic}` (only if they really posted; "re your post" is not a fake Re:)

### 8. Result / proof (only if real)
- `how {similar_business} got 23 more bookings`
- `{town} cafe, 2x pickup orders`

### 9. Ultra-short / personal
- `{first_name}?`
- `{business}`
- `idea for {business}`

## Picking the winner
Score each 1-5 on:
1. Specific to them
2. Curiosity strength
3. Honesty (body pays it off)
4. Looks human (not marketing)
Recommend highest total. Ties -> shortest.

## Preview text (first line of body)
Inbox shows subject + ~90 chars of body. Make line 1:
- Continue the curiosity, not repeat subject
- Never "Hi, my name is" or "I hope you're well"
- Example: subject `your 3rd google review` -> preview `"Called twice, nobody picked up" - that's from Priya last Tuesday. Happens to every busy...`

## Follow-up subjects
Default: reply in same thread (keeps context, better deliverability). If new thread required, use a new angle: `one more idea for {business}`, `{business} - closing the loop`.
