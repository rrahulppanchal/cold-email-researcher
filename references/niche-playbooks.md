## SaaS

**Reader**: founder (early) or Head of Growth/Marketing (later). Skims in 5 seconds. Respects data and brevity. Hates agency pitches.

**Pains**
- Homepage doesn't say what it does in 5 seconds; visitors bounce
- Trial signups don't activate; onboarding emails generic or missing
- Pricing page confusing (unclear units, no annual toggle, no local currency)
- No case studies / social proof; logos only
- Demo booking friction (form -> email -> back-and-forth instead of instant calendar)
- Blog dead or not ranking; competitors own comparison keywords ("X vs Y", "X alternative")
- Small team drowning in manual work: lead routing, CRM updates, support triage, churn alerts
- Slow site (Lighthouse < 50 mobile), broken links, outdated screenshots

**What to look for**: hero headline clarity, CTA count, signup steps, pricing page, "/vs" and "/alternatives" pages, last blog post date, changelog cadence, G2 complaints, job posts.

**Offers that land**
- Homepage/landing page rewrite + redesign for conversion
- Comparison/alternative landing pages for SEO
- Onboarding + lifecycle email automation
- Instant demo booking + lead enrichment + CRM routing (n8n/Zapier/Make)
- AI support assistant trained on docs
- Churn-risk alerts to Slack

**Their words**: signups, trials, activation, demo, pipeline, conversion, churn, MRR, ICP, PLG.

**Hook examples**
- "Your homepage headline says 'The future of work, reimagined' - took me 3 scrolls to learn you do invoice approval for agencies."
- "Searched 'Asana alternative for agencies' - 4 of your competitors have a page for it. You don't."
- "Two G2 reviews this month say setup took 'days'. That's usually an onboarding email gap, not a product gap."
- "Saw you're hiring an SDR - before that hire, a demo-routing automation might free up half that role."

## Cafes and coffee shops

**Reader**: owner, often also behind the counter. Reads on phone, evenings. Proud of their coffee, food, vibe.

**Pains**
- No website or menu only on Instagram/as a blurry photo
- Not showing on Google Maps for "cafe near me" / "best coffee in [area]"
- No online ordering or pre-order for pickup; morning rush line loses customers
- Catering/event/large order enquiries buried in DMs
- Quiet afternoons/weekdays; no loyalty or email/WhatsApp list to bring regulars back
- Hours wrong on Google; reviews unanswered

**What to look for**: Google rating + count, review mentions of "wait", "line", "closed when it said open"; Instagram last post; link in bio; menu format; competitor cafes nearby.

**Offers**: one-page mobile site with menu + map + hours; pickup pre-ordering; loyalty/WhatsApp/SMS list automation; Google profile optimization; auto review-request after purchase; catering enquiry form.

**Their words**: regulars, rush, footfall, quiet afternoons, pickup, catering, menu, vibe.

**Hook examples**
- "Your menu on Google is a photo from 2022 - the oat flat white is still ₹180 on it."
- "Three reviews mention the morning line. Pre-order for pickup usually fixes that and nobody walks away."
- "Searched 'best cafe in Koramangala' - you're not in the top 3 map results, and you've got better reviews than #2."

## Restaurants, bakeries, cloud kitchens

**Pains**: paying 20-30% commission to Swiggy/Zomato/UberEats/DoorDash; no direct ordering; no table booking; menu PDF unreadable on phone; no repeat-customer list; catering/party enquiries lost; custom cake orders via endless DMs (bakery).

**Offers**: direct ordering site (commission-free), table reservation, digital menu, WhatsApp/SMS reorder campaigns, custom order form with deposit, review automation.

**Hook examples**
- "If you're doing even 40 delivery orders a day on Zomato, that commission is roughly [estimate] a month - a direct-order link on your Instagram claws some of that back."
- "Custom cake orders are going through Instagram DMs - a simple order form with photos and deposit would save you hours of back-and-forth."

## Lawn care and landscaping

**Reader**: owner-operator, often in the truck all day. Checks email at night. Business is seasonal - spring rush, fall cleanup, winter slow (snow removal maybe).

**Pains**
- Missed calls while on a job = lost quotes (biggest pain)
- No online quote request; customers want instant estimate
- Site lacks before/after photos, service area, prices from
- Not ranking for "lawn care [town]" / "landscaper near me"
- Scheduling and recurring-service reminders done by hand
- Few Google reviews vs competitors; no system to ask
- Invoicing/payment chasing

**What to look for**: Google review count vs top competitor, reviews mentioning "never called back", site has quote form?, photos, service area pages, seasonal services listed, Facebook activity, Nextdoor mentions.

**Offers**: quote-request site with photo upload; missed-call text-back automation; auto review requests after job; recurring service reminders; service-area pages for SEO; seasonal campaign emails ("spring cleanup slots open").

**Their words**: jobs, quotes, estimates, crews, routes, the season, spring rush, cleanups, mowing, curb appeal.

**Hook examples**
- "Two of your recent Google reviews say 'hard to reach' - when you're on a mower, calls go to voicemail and that quote goes to the next guy."
- "Searched 'lawn care Frisco' - GreenScape has an instant-quote form and 140 reviews; you have better photos but only a phone number."
- "Spring rush starts in ~6 weeks. Want a quote form live before the phones start ringing?"

## Home services: plumbers, HVAC, electricians, roofers

**Pains**: emergency calls missed after-hours = biggest loss (one missed job $300-$5,000+); no online booking; no "emergency 24/7" clarity; weak Google ranking; no review system; slow follow-up on quotes; dispatch chaos.

**Offers**: missed-call text-back + after-hours AI answering/booking; service + city landing pages; online booking; quote follow-up automation; review requests; maintenance-plan reminders (HVAC tune-ups).

**Hook examples**
- "Your site says 'call for emergencies' but the number goes to voicemail after 6pm - I tried at 8."  (only if actually tested and true)
- "AC season ends in a few weeks - a tune-up reminder automation to last year's customers fills the slow months."

## Cleaning services

**Pains**: quote back-and-forth; no instant pricing; recurring booking managed by text; cancellations; no reviews system; Facebook-only presence.

**Offers**: instant-quote calculator by sq ft/rooms; online recurring booking + payment; reminder texts; review automation.

**Hook**: "Your Facebook page says 'message for a quote' - most people want a price in 30 seconds or they message the next cleaner."

## Salons, barbers, spas

**Pains**: bookings via phone/DM; no-shows; empty weekday slots; no rebooking reminders; Instagram is the only "website"; reviews mention wait times.

**Offers**: online booking with deposits, no-show reminders, rebooking nudges ("it's been 5 weeks since your cut"), last-minute slot alerts, simple site with services + prices + gallery.

**Hook**: "You're booking through Instagram DMs - each no-show is a dead slot. Deposits + SMS reminders usually cut those in half." (label estimate)

## Gyms, yoga and fitness studios

**Pains**: trial leads not followed up; class booking clunky; membership churn; no lead nurture; schedule only as image.

**Offers**: free-trial landing page + instant follow-up sequence, class booking, win-back automation for lapsed members, referral system.

**Hook**: "Your 'free trial' form just says 'we'll get back to you' - the gyms winning on this text within 5 minutes."

---

## Dentists, clinics, physios, chiropractors

**Pains**: phone-only booking; reception overloaded; no-shows; recall reminders manual; outdated site; reviews mention "couldn't get through".

**Offers**: online booking, recall/reminder automation, AI receptionist for FAQs and after-hours, new-patient landing pages, review automation. Keep tone professional; be careful with health data (no patient data in examples; mention compliance like HIPAA where relevant).

**Hook**: "Four reviews mention 'couldn't get through on the phone' - online booking takes that load off the front desk."

---

## Auto repair and detailing

**Pains**: quotes by phone only, no booking, no service reminders, few reviews, detailing packages unclear.

**Offers**: booking with package picker, oil-change/service reminders, before/after gallery, review automation.

## Real estate agents

**Pains**: leads from portals not followed up fast; no personal brand site; listings only on portals; no nurture for long-cycle buyers.

**Offers**: personal site with IDX/listings, instant lead response automation, drip nurture, valuation landing page ("What's my home worth in [area]").

**Hook**: "Searched your name - Zillow profile comes up first, not you. That means portal owns the relationship."

## Other local service businesses (catch-all)

Apply the core local pains checklist:
1. Can a customer find them on Google for "[service] near [town]"?
2. Can they understand services + rough prices in 10 seconds on a phone?
3. Can they book/order/request a quote without calling?
4. What happens to a missed call or after-hours enquiry?
5. Is there a system to get reviews and bring past customers back?

Whichever answer is worst = the hook.
