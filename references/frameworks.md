# Email Frameworks and Templates

Templates are skeletons, not copy to paste. Every bracket must become a real, researched specific. If a bracket can't be filled with something true, pick another framework.

## 1. Observation -> Cost -> Fix -> Ask

```
Hi [Name],

[Specific observation about their business, sourced.]

[What that likely costs them, in their terms. Soft estimate labelled.]

[What you'd do + one proof line.]

[Yes/no ask]?

[Your name]
[Business] · [one link]

P.S. [second hook or proof]
```

## 2. Review mining (local - very strong)

Quote or paraphrase a real public review that shows a fixable problem. Be kind - the problem is the system, not them.

```
Hi [Name],

"[short quote from review]" - that's from a Google review on [day/month].

Not a knock on [Business] - it happens to every busy [niche] when the [phone/DMs/forms] are the only way in. Usually means [X] customers a week quietly go elsewhere.

I set up [fix: online booking / missed-call text-back] for [similar business] - [real result or "takes about a week"].

Want me to show you how it'd look for [Business]?

[Name]
```

## 3. Competitor gap

```
Hi [Name],

Googled "[service] [town]" this morning. [Competitor] shows up first with [thing they have: online quotes / booking / 140 reviews]. [Business] shows up [position] with [what you have].

Your [reviews/photos/work] look better - you're just harder to [book/find/order from].

[Fix in one line.] I can have it live in [timeframe].

Worth a quick look?
```

## 4. Missed-call / after-hours math

```
Hi [Name],

Called [Business] at [time] [day] - went to voicemail. (Totally normal when you're on a job.)

Most people don't leave a message; they call the next [niche] on Google. If that's even 2 jobs a week at ~[avg ticket], that's [estimate]/month walking away.

I set up a system that instantly texts back missed callers with a booking link - they're "handled" in 10 seconds even while you're [on a roof / under a sink].

Want me to send a 2-min video of how it works?
```
Only use "I called" if the user actually called/tested it. Otherwise phrase as "when you're on a job, calls likely go to voicemail".

## 6. Mockup / loom give-first

```
Hi [Name],

I made a quick [mockup / 2-min loom] of what [Business]'s [homepage / booking page] could look like with [specific improvement].

[One sentence why it matters to them.]

Want me to send it over? No strings.

[Name]
```
Curiosity gap: don't put the link in email 1 - ask permission. Better deliverability and a reply starts the conversation.

## 7. Trigger event

```
Hi [Name],

Congrats on [new location / funding / launch / award] - [specific detail].

[What usually breaks next at that stage, tied to their site/ops.] e.g. "Second location usually means the booking page and Google profiles need to split, or calls land at the wrong shop."

[Fix + proof.]

Open to a 10-min chat this week?
```

## 8. No website at all (local)

```
Hi [Name],

[Business] has [4.7 stars from 180 reviews] on Google - but no website link. When someone taps "Website" on your listing, nothing happens.

People comparing [niche]s in [town] usually check the menu/prices/photos before [visiting/calling]. Right now [competitor] gets that click.

I build simple one-page sites for [niche]s - [menu/services, hours, map, book/order button] - live in about a week.

Want me to mock one up for you, free?
```

## 9. Follow-ups (reply in thread)

Email touches of the multi-channel sequence. Days 3, 7, 12, 16 are email; DMs, calls, and social touches in between are in `campaigns.md` §4.

**Day 3 - new angle**
```
Hi [Name], one more thing I noticed - [backup finding, 1 line]. [Why it matters, 1 line]. Want both fixes in one quick plan?
```

**Day 7 - proof / give**
```
Hi [Name], [similar business in their niche/town] had the same [problem]. After [fix], [real result]. Happy to send the [mockup/loom] I made for [Business] - want it?
```
(If no real case study: "I went ahead and sketched the [booking page] for [Business] - want me to send it?")

**Day 12 - fresh value**
```
Hi [Name], quick one - [seasonal tip / competitor change / new idea relevant to them, 1-2 lines]. Still happy to send the [asset] if useful.
```

**Day 16 - breakup**
```
Hi [Name], don't want to clutter your inbox. Should I close the loop on this? If timing's just off, happy to check back before [season/quarter].
```

## 10. Worked examples

### Local - lawn care (review mining)
Subject: `your google review from march`
```
Hi Mike,

"Great work but took 3 tries to get someone on the phone" - that's from a Google review on Mike's Lawn Co. in March.

Not surprising when you're out mowing all day. But most people won't try 3 times - they call the next guy on the list.

I set up missed-call text-back + an online quote form for lawn crews. Caller gets a text in 10 seconds with a quote link, even while you're on a job.

Want me to show you what it'd look like for Mike's?

Sam
Greenline Web · greenlineweb.com

P.S. You've got 4.8 stars - just 31 reviews though. Same system can ask happy customers for reviews automatically.
```
(78 words body - good)

```
Your reviews are honestly better (4.7 vs 4.4). People just can't see your menu or order ahead.

I build one-page cafe sites with menu + pickup ordering - live in a week.

Want a free mockup for Bean There?

Alex
```

```
Jordan
```
